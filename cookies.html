Cookie Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

The short version

  • Our code sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
  • The company that hosts this site may set a strictly necessary cookie to protect it from bots and attacks.
  • That's why there's no cookie banner. If we ever add cookies that need your consent, we'll ask first.

What our pages do

Spawnbook's pages set no cookies. They use no analytics, ad tags, tracking pixels, local storage or session storage. The calculator and the request forms work only in your browser's memory, and everything you type is gone when you close or reload the page.

Cookies from our host

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, run by Cloudflare, Inc. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary cookie to protect the site from bots and attacks. It isn't used for advertising or tracking. We don't control that cookie and can't see what's in it. Cloudflare's Cookie Policy (opens in a new tab) explains how it's used.

Fonts

Our pages use typefaces stored on this site, so loading them doesn't send anything to a font service.

Do you need to agree to cookies?

No. Because our code sets no cookies, there's nothing for you to accept or reject, so the site shows no cookie banner. The host's cookies are covered by the host's own policy and controls.

If we ever add analytics or any other cookies that aren't strictly needed to run the site, we'll update this policy first and ask for your consent wherever the law requires it.

Controlling cookies yourself

Every major browser lets you see, block and delete cookies in its settings. Blocking the host's security cookie may stop some pages from loading.
