Track record

Every Monday we log the games on the board, then read them again straight from Roblox for eight weeks. Here's how they did, next to every other game that made our cut that day but wasn't picked.

"Players" is the live player count at our daily reading, about 2:30 PM Pacific. One reading can swing with the time of day or a weekend, so we compare medians: half the games did better than the number shown, half did worse.

"Others that made the cut" are the games that passed the same filters that day (under four months old, 500 to 15,000 players, rated 85% or better) but didn't score high enough for the board. If our picks beat them, the scoring is finding something the filters alone don't.
