Cookie Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

The short version

  • Our code sets cookies only to keep you signed in, to run your free day, and for a few minutes while you sign in with Google. None of them are for ads or tracking.
  • We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies.
  • The company that hosts this site may set a strictly necessary cookie to protect it from bots and attacks.
  • Both are strictly necessary, so there's no cookie banner. If we ever add cookies that need your consent, we'll ask first.

What our pages do

When you sign up or sign in, we set one cookie named sb_session. It holds a random code that keeps you signed in so you can open the board, and it expires after 60 days or when you sign out. It isn't used for analytics or advertising, and you can delete it at any time; you'd just need to sign in again.

If you choose Continue with Google, we set a cookie named sb_oauth for up to 10 minutes. It holds random check codes and the page to send you back to, so we can tell that the answer from Google belongs to the sign-in you started. If Google signs you in with an email that doesn't have an account yet, a cookie named sb_gnew holds a random code for up to 15 minutes while you finish your account. Both are deleted as soon as you're signed in.

When you open the board without an account, we set a cookie named sb_trial. It holds the time your free 24 hours started, signed so it can't be changed, and nothing about you. It lets you use the board for those 24 hours and remembers for 30 days that this browser has had its free day. It isn't used for analytics or advertising.

Apart from these cookies, Spawnbook's pages use no ad tags or tracking pixels. If you sign up for the daily email, your browser's local storage keeps one note, sb_daily_email set to 1, so the board doesn't ask you again. It holds nothing about you, and clearing your browser's site data removes it. The pages use no other local storage or session storage. The calculator, the board's game finder and the request forms work only in your browser's memory, and everything you type is gone when you close or reload the page, unless you choose to save your game finder answers to your account.

Visit counts

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and see which sites send people here. Cloudflare says it sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser for this, and it doesn't fingerprint visitors. We see only totals. The Privacy Policy lists what it records.

Cookies from our host

This site is hosted on Cloudflare, run by Cloudflare, Inc. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary cookie to protect the site from bots and attacks. It isn't used for advertising or tracking. We don't control that cookie and can't see what's in it. Cloudflare's Cookie Policy (opens in a new tab) explains how it's used.

Fonts

Our pages use typefaces stored on this site, so loading them doesn't send anything to a font service.

Do you need to agree to cookies?

No. The sign-in cookie is strictly necessary to give you the access you asked for, so it doesn't need your consent, and the site shows no cookie banner. The host's cookies are covered by the host's own policy and controls.

If we ever add cookies that aren't strictly needed to run the site, we'll update this policy first and ask for your consent wherever the law requires it.

Controlling cookies yourself

Every major browser lets you see, block and delete cookies in its settings. Blocking the host's security cookie may stop some pages from loading.
