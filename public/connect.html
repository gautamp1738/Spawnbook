Prove your game's numbers

Connect a read-only key from Roblox and get a link showing your game's real players and Robux, read straight from Roblox. Share it with a buyer, a studio or a partner instead of screenshots, which anyone can fake.

What the key can do
Read your game's analytics: players, Robux earned, retention. Nothing else.
What it can't do
Change your game, see your password, or touch your Robux or your account.
What we do with it
Use it once to read your last 90 days, then throw it away. We never save it. Delete it on Roblox when you're done.
What it costs
Nothing, and you don't need an account.

Takes about three minutes. You'll need to be the game's owner, or a group admin who can manage its settings.

Make your key

Keep this page open in one tab and Roblox in another. Copy each bit of text exactly; the names on Roblox are easy to mix up.

  1. Open the API keys page on Roblox

    If a group owns the game, switch to that group at the top of Creator Hub first, so the key belongs to the group.

    Open Roblox API keys (opens in a new tab)

  2. Click Create API Key and name it

    Call it something you'll recognize, like Spawnbook, so it's easy to delete later.

  3. Add the analytics system

    Under Access Permissions, type this into Select API System and pick it:

    universe-analytics

    Not universe-analytics-dashboard. That one has a similar name and won't work.

  4. Pick your game and the read permission

    Select your game, then add this as the only operation:

    universe.analytics:read

  5. Allow our server to use it

    Under Security, add this as the accepted IP address. Our server's address changes, so a fixed one wouldn't work. The key still only reads analytics, and it stops working when it expires.

    0.0.0.0/0

  6. Set it to expire, then copy it

    Pick an expiration a day or a week away. Click Save & Generate Key and copy the key right away: Roblox only shows it once.

Connect your game

Never paste your password, your account cookie or a key with other permissions here, or anywhere else. Nobody needs them to check your numbers.
