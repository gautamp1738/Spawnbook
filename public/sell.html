Open to selling your game?

Tell us about it. We match games with studios and investors who are buying now, and we only pass your details to a buyer after you say yes.

  1. You tell us about your game with the form below.
  2. If it fits what a buyer wants, we message you and ask for a short screen recording of your Creator Dashboard, so buyers see real numbers.
  3. With your OK, we send your contact and those numbers to that buyer. You talk to them directly and decide everything yourself.

It's free for owners, and we don't take a cut of any sale. Not sure what it's worth? Value your game first.
