What verified means

  • The owner told us they'd sell, and said we could share their contact and numbers with you.
  • We've seen their Creator Dashboard for the game, in a screen recording or live, and checked it against Roblox's public numbers.
  • We take no cut of the deal. You talk to the owner directly, and we don't take part in the sale.

Verified isn't a guarantee. Recordings can be faked and games change fast, so check the numbers yourself before you pay, put the deal in writing and use escrow. See how a sale works.
